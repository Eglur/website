Know

A complete, sourced picture.

Everything the office has done, what is happening now, and who is involved, filled from public sources before the first login.

Personal record

Every speech, question and answer, committee question, bill, amendment and vote, complete from the first login. Questions are tracked to their answers, and promises the member makes become follow-ups.

Developments

Legislation, government policy, parliamentary business, council papers and the news, filtered to the issues you follow. A morning briefing in your office’s own format, a weekly look-ahead, and a check of any figure against the official source.

Constituency profile

The area in official figures, down to ward level where published: population, health, economy, housing, transport and public services. Public data only.

Contacts

Who the office deals with, about what, and what was promised, built from correspondence and meetings and confirmed with one click.

Library

Everything the office has written and agreed, searchable by meaning, with answers that cite the exact documents.

Decide

One short list, with the reasons.

Eglur ranks by reasons a person can read, never by a hidden score, and keeps every topic’s history on one page.

Today

What needs you now, readable in under 30 seconds: decisions waiting, today’s engagements with a brief for each, and the developments that matter.

Priorities you can read

Every item says why it ranks where it does: the issue it matches, the member’s record, local impact and timing. Raise, lower or mute anything with one click. Nothing is hidden.

Issues

One page per topic, with its whole history, the agreed line, open questions and next steps.

Do

Finished work, approved by a person.

Most work starts itself, and nothing leaves Eglur until someone in the office approves it.

Work

Tasks with an owner, a deadline and an issue, mostly suggested from context. Procedural deadlines are built in, and replies you’re waiting on are chased.

Drafting

Questions, letters, speeches, briefings and statements drafted from your agreed lines and checked sources. Every factual claim carries its source.

Inbox

Your mailbox sorted where it lives, with labels in Outlook. Possible casework is labelled and left untouched.

Diary

A brief before every meeting, and follow-ups captured after it in a sentence or a voice note. Private entries stay private.

Comms

Statements and posts drafted from agreed lines, in Welsh and English where needed, and approved before anything is sent.

Checked drafts

Every draft shows its working.

Each sentence says where it came from. Facts carry their source and are checked against it. AI suggestions stay in italics until a person accepts them. Select a sentence to see its checks.

Across everything

From the first login to the last day in office.

Capabilities that run through every module, for one person or a whole team.

Useful in 10 minutes

Eglur arrives already filled from public sources. Three choices to set up, and no training.

Ask Eglur

One box, or one voice note, for any question or instruction. Answers cite their sources, and every action comes back as a proposal.

On the member’s phone

Approvals, meeting briefs, search and voice capture between meetings, with passkey sign-in.

Continuity

Nothing is lost when staff change. When a member leaves, their data goes with them, and a consensual handover pack helps a successor start well.

Your controls

Roles, approvals, sources, retention, Welsh or English, a full export at any time and the office’s own audit log.

Time returned, measured

A short weekly note shows the estimated hours returned to the office as a whole, never individual staff activity.

The Eglur standard

Rules every feature follows.

A feature that breaks one of these is changed or removed.

  • Give before asking.

    Eglur arrives already filled in. There are no required fields: it suggests, and people confirm.

  • Return more time than it takes.

    Every feature must save more time than it costs, measured on real office tasks.

  • Explain everything.

    Every ranking, suggestion and draft shows why, and where its facts came from.

  • Quiet by default.

    One briefing a day, and interruptions only for what must be done today. No streaks or badges.

  • Solo first.

    Everything works for one person. Team features appear only when there is a team.

  • Work where people already work.

    Outlook, email and the phone come first. Eglur adds to the tools you have.

Where we are

Built carefully, in stages.

No real office data enters Eglur until an independent security review is passed.

Under wayPlanned

  1. Under way: Now

    Building the foundations with public data only, and talking to offices about how their work runs.

  2. Planned: Early 2027

    First offices try Eglur on public data: briefings, developments and drafting from the public record. An independent security review runs alongside.

  3. Planned: Summer 2027

    Pilots with office data, once the security review is passed, measuring the time returned.

  4. Planned: Late 2027 onwards

    Wider availability, councils, and the handover pack.

Design partners

Shape Eglur with us.

We’re inviting a small number of offices to use Eglur from early 2027, before anyone else, and to shape what it does.

What you get.

Free use throughout the pilot, features shaped around how your office works, and a direct line to the founder.

What we ask.

A short feedback call every few weeks, and honest views on what saves time and what doesn’t.

Public data only to start. Your office walled off from every other, and a written data agreement before any office data is used.
