Questions

Where does it come from?

Official public records: UK Parliament, the Senedd and your council. Every item links to its source.

Is it written by AI?

AI picks what's relevant and explains it. It can make mistakes, so every summary links to the official record.

Is it political?

No. It covers what every party and representative does, and doesn't tell you what to think.

What happens to my email?

We use it only to tell you when Eglur Daily launches. We never share it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
