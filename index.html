Eglur Technologies · In development

Clarity for public servants.

Eglur is the workspace for the offices of elected representatives. One secure record of everything the office knows and does, and each morning, a short list of what needs attention, with the reasons and the sources.

Eglur is Welsh for clear.

Mission

Take the admin. Give back the human work.

Public service is human work: judgement, knowledge, care and relationships. Too much of that time goes on slow administration and scattered information. Eglur exists to give it back, so people spend more of their working day with each other, and less with screens.

Hours given back.

Our measure of success is time returned to public servants each week, with the quality of their work intact. Success means less time in Eglur, not more.

A person always decides.

Eglur proposes and keeps the evidence. Nothing is published or sent until someone in the office approves it.

Built for the office alone.

Independent and impartial. Eglur works only for the office using it, and shows no paid content from anyone else.

Who it’s for

For the offices that represent us.

Built first for the staff who support elected representatives, and for representatives who work alone.

MPs’ offices.

Westminster business and procedure, questions routed to the right department, and a constituency team often working miles away.

Parliamentary teams

Members of the devolved parliaments.

Senedd, Scottish Parliament and Northern Ireland Assembly offices, with Welsh and English side by side where chosen.

Devolved offices

Councillors.

Council papers, committees and wards, designed to work for one person with no staff at all.

How it works

One record. One short list.

Everything the office knows connects to the issues it follows. Eglur turns that into what needs attention today, and helps finish the work.

01

Know.

The member’s full record, the day’s developments from official sources and the news, the constituency in figures, and the office’s own memory.

02

Decide.

Today shows what needs you now in under 30 seconds, with the reasons for every priority and a link to every source.

03

Do.

Questions, letters, briefings and statements drafted from your agreed lines and checked sources, then approved by a person.

No required fields, no training, and quiet by default.

Trust

Your office’s data stays your office’s.

Commitments written down before the first office signs up, and published in full.

Each office walled off.

Separated inside the database itself, across and within parties.

Nothing kept by the AI.

Zero data retention and no training on your data. The AI drafts; a person decides.

Kept in the UK.

Hosted in the UK and encrypted, with every supplier published before the first pilot.

No casework collected.

Constituents’ cases stay in your casework system. Eglur never collects them.

Security and privacy, designed in from the first line of code.
