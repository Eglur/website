Our mission

Public service is human work.

Judgement, knowledge, care and relationships. Too much of that time is lost to slow administration, scattered information and disorganisation. Eglur exists to give it back, so people spend more of their working day with each other, and less with screens.

Hours given back

Our measure of success is time returned to public servants each week, with the quality of their work intact. Success means less time in Eglur, not more.

A person always decides

Eglur proposes and keeps the evidence. Nothing is published or sent until someone in the office approves it.

Built for the office alone

Independent and impartial. Eglur works only for the office using it, and shows no paid content from anyone else.

How it works

Brief. Draft. Approve.

One workspace arranged around the issues an office cares about, from the morning briefing to the Member's sign-off.

01

Brief

A morning briefing on what's moving, what others are saying, and what needs a decision, each item with the reason it's there.

02

Draft

Questions, statements and posts drafted from the office's own agreed lines and record, never a blank page, with every fact sourced.

03

Approve

The representative reviews and signs off, at a desk or on a phone, and the record keeps what was said and why.

Fact

Plain text, always with its source.

AI reading

Italic, until a person checks it.

Agreed line

“The representative's own words.”

AI proposes. Eglur keeps the evidence. The human decides.
