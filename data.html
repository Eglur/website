Trust and data

How Eglur handles your office's data.

The plain-English version. Eglur is in development, so these are the commitments every office will get, written down before the first one signs up.

Stays in your workspace

Your issues, agreed lines, drafts, diary and record. Visible only to people your office invites.

Sent to the AI, then forgotten

Only the text needed for a task, under terms where the AI provider keeps nothing and trains on nothing.

Never collected

Constituents' casework, health, housing or immigration details. Casework stays in your casework system.

Your office stays in control

Your office is the data controller. Eglur processes your material only on your instructions, under a written data processing agreement signed before any use.

No training, no storage by the AI provider

Eglur uses commercial terms with zero data retention: the AI provider does not store your prompts or use them to train its models.

Any narrow exception, such as abuse monitoring required by law, will be stated here in full.

Every office kept separate

Each office's material is walled off at the database level, so one office can never see another's, across or within parties.

Kept in the UK, encrypted

Data is hosted in the UK and encrypted in transit and at rest. A list of every sub-processor, and where it operates, will be published here.

Who can see what

Sign-in requires two-step verification. Inside Eglur, very few people can reach production systems, and every administrative action is logged. Support access to an office happens only with its permission, for a limited time.

Yours to keep, yours to delete

Choose how long material is kept and delete records at any time. When a contract ends, or an office changes hands after an election, you receive a full export and everything is then deleted.

A person always decides

Nothing is published or sent automatically. AI text is marked as such until someone in the office checks it.

Clear bills

One flat monthly price per office, with invoices that state the business purpose. No usage-based surprises. Eglur is for office work, not party campaigning.

Questions about data? Write to security@eglurtech.com. Last updated November 2026.
