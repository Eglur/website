Trust and data

How Eglur handles your office’s data.

The plain-English version. Eglur is in development, so these are the commitments every office will get, written down before the first one signs up.

Last updated 28 November 2026 · Questions: security@eglurtech.com

The principle

Eglur holds only what serves the office, for as long as the office wants it, and never uses one office’s data for anyone else.

Stays in your workspace

Your issues, agreed lines, drafts, diary and record. Visible only to the people your office invites.

Sent to the AI, then forgotten

Only the text a task needs, under terms where the AI provider keeps nothing and trains on nothing.

Never collected

Constituents’ casework, and health, housing or immigration details. Casework stays in your casework system.

What Eglur holds

Different kinds of data are treated differently. An office’s own history is the memory that makes Eglur useful, so it is kept, and kept safe.

Kind of dataExamplesHow Eglur treats it
Public recordQuestions, debate contributions, votes, published answersUsed freely: it is already public.
Your office’s own workBriefings, agreed lines, decisions, meeting notesKept as your office’s memory. You own it and set how long it is kept.
ContactsStakeholders’ names, roles, organisations and past dealingsKept for a clear purpose. Anyone can ask what is held about them.
Constituent caseworkIndividual cases, including health, housing and immigrationNot held. Later, only in stages, after legal review and independent security checks.

Your office stays in control

  • Your office is the data controller. Eglur processes your material only on your instructions, under a written data processing agreement signed before any use.
  • A data protection impact assessment covers how Eglur works, and is shared with your office.
  • You choose how long each kind of data is kept, with sensible defaults.
  • A full export at any time, in open formats.
  • Delete any record at any time. When a contract ends, you receive a full export, everything is then deleted, and it leaves the backups as they expire.

Every office kept separate

  • Walled off inside the database itself, so one office can never see another’s material, across or within parties.
  • Nothing is pooled across offices. No office’s data is used to improve another office’s service.
  • Sensitive issues and documents can be restricted to named roles, such as the member and chief of staff.
  • Diaries stay private. The member’s private entries show only as busy, and meeting locations are visible only to roles that need them.
  • Planned: Separate encryption keys for each office. Before wider availability

Signing in and access

  • Passkeys from day one: sign-in that can’t be phished, and is easier than a password.
  • Sessions expire, and a lost phone or laptop can be signed out remotely in one step.
  • Leavers lose access immediately when they leave the office.
  • Eglur’s own team has no standing access to your data. Support access needs your office’s permission, is limited in time, and appears in your audit log.
  • A tamper-evident audit log records who did what and when, and your office can read it.
  • Planned: Single sign-on with Microsoft work accounts. Planned

How Eglur uses AI

AI drafts and summarises. People decide. These rules hold for every AI request Eglur makes.

  • One gateway for every AI request, so each one can be logged, tested and controlled in one place.
  • Zero data retention: the AI provider stores nothing and trains on nothing. Any narrow exception, such as abuse monitoring required by law, will be stated here in full.
  • Only the text a task needs is sent, never contacts, diaries or case details it doesn’t need.
  • Answers cite their sources, and Eglur checks that each citation points to a real document.
  • Text from the news, documents and emails is treated as information, never as instructions, so hidden commands in a web page or an email can’t steer the AI.
  • The AI proposes and never acts. AI text stays marked as AI until a person checks it, and nothing is sent or published without approval.
  • If an AI provider is unavailable, everything except drafting keeps working, and providers can be switched.

Where your data lives

  • Hosted in the UK, and encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Backups are encrypted, and restored regularly to prove they work.
  • No advertising, no data sales, and no paid or sponsored content.
  • Planned: Every supplier listed: each company that processes your data, what it does and where it operates, published on this page. Before the first pilot

Casework

  • The first release holds no constituent casework.
  • Possible casework is left alone. If Eglur sees mail that looks like an individual’s case, it labels it and leaves it in your mailbox, untouched and never imported.
  • Planned: Casework support in stages: first links to your existing casework system, then anonymised themes, then fuller support, each only after legal review and independent security checks. Later

When people and seats change

  • Data belongs to the member, not the seat. When a member leaves, their office receives a full export, and the data is then deleted.
  • A successor never inherits a predecessor’s data automatically, whichever party either belongs to.
  • Election periods: Eglur keeps office work apart from campaigning, and switches to the limited mode the rules allow.
  • Planned: A consensual handover pack: the outgoing member chooses what passes on, such as non-partisan local issues, official contacts and ongoing public matters. Open cases move only with each constituent’s consent. Strategy, private positions, internal notes and staff records never pass on. Later

Impartial by design

  • The same product and the same default sources for every party.
  • Eglur adds no views of its own. Drafts follow the office’s own agreed lines.
  • No connection to party campaign databases, in either direction.
  • For office work, not party campaigning.

Security we can show

  • Measured against the NCSC’s cloud security principles.
  • An independent penetration test before any real office data, and before each major release.
  • Ready for the worst day: continuous monitoring, and an incident plan in place before launch. Affected offices are told without delay, with what they need to report to the ICO within 72 hours.
  • Careful code: every change reviewed and automatically checked, dependencies scanned, and no secrets kept in code.
  • Planned: Cyber Essentials, then Cyber Essentials Plus, then ISO 27001. From 2027
  • Planned: A bug bounty that rewards outside researchers for reporting flaws. Later

Your rights

  • Anyone whose details Eglur holds as a contact can ask what is held, correct it, or ask for it to be removed.
  • Subject access requests made easy: a person’s record can be exported in minutes, so offices can answer well within the legal time limit.
  • Requests go to the office that holds the data, or to security@eglurtech.com.

Clear bills

  • One flat monthly price per office, with no usage-based surprises.
  • Invoices state the business purpose, to suit IPSA and council rules.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no trackers.
  • Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.
  • Hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Email to eglurtech.com addresses is handled by Microsoft 365.
  • Messages sent with the contact form are stored in our database (Supabase) and a private Google Sheet, and emailed to us. Only we can see them. We use them only to reply to you, and delete them if you ask.

Report a security issue

If you think you’ve found a vulnerability in Eglur or this website, email security@eglurtech.com.

  • Give us reasonable time to fix it before sharing it.
  • Don’t access, change or keep data that isn’t yours.
  • Good faith is protected: we won’t take action against research that follows these rules.
  • A machine-readable contact is at /.well-known/security.txt.
